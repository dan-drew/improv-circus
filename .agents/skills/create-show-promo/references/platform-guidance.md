# Platform production baseline

Research date: 2026-10-11. This is an embedded production baseline, not a promise of universal placement compatibility. Do not routinely browse on each use. Recheck official guidance when the user requests an update or a platform rejects/crops an export. Account-level publishing previews remain necessary before posting/boosting.

## Canvas and safe rectangles

Coordinates are pixels from the top-left, with right/bottom exclusive. Export sRGB PNG. Keep Meta files below 30 MB as a production cap.

| Deliverable | Canvas | Essential-content rectangle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook post | 1440 × 1800, 4:5 | x 90–1350, y 225–1575 |
| Instagram post | 1440 × 1800, 4:5 | x 90–1350, y 225–1575 |
| Facebook Reel artwork | 1440 × 2560, 9:16 | x 100–1340, y 360–1536 |
| Instagram Reel artwork | 1440 × 2560, 9:16 | x 100–1340, y 360–1664 |
| TikTok vertical artwork | 1080 × 1920, 9:16 | x 120–840, y 300–1150 |

Use 4:5 for the feed deliverables to prioritize mobile screen area. The feed rectangle adds generous side padding and keeps essential content within the centered square as protection against square previews; it is a house layout rule, not an official platform safe zone. A separately requested square campaign should be recomposed, not blindly cropped.

Instagram Reels use a 35% bottom reserve for standard ads and boosted promos, with approximately 14% top and 7% sides as working production baselines. The top/side measurements have not been independently verified from the live Meta pages. Facebook Reels retain the conservative 40% bottom baseline until placement-specific guidance or an account preview supports reducing it. Increase a reserve only when the actual preview, disclaimer or additional overlay requires it; do not add extra padding automatically. Organic Reels have no separately verified fixed rectangle here: use the working baseline and inspect the actual organic preview rather than describing ad recommendations as universal organic requirements.

Use the available safe width: make show identity, date and guest imagery large and readable across the permitted region. Reject layouts with large unused internal side margins. Measure the combined essential-content bounds against the available rectangle and review at phone size; percentage compliance alone does not establish visual quality. Recompose the elements rather than uniformly shrinking a complete poster into a small center box. Background and decorative audience imagery should fill the canvas, including interface reserves.

TikTok's rectangle reserves substantial room for top UI, right action rail, captions, and bottom CTA. It is a house approximation, not TikTok's official fixed boundary. Decorative elements can bleed; logos, dates, text, guest faces and required marks cannot.

An image's safe rectangle does not guarantee its cover/profile-grid crop: preview those surfaces separately, choose a manual cover crop, and preserve date/show identity where possible. All details must be readable in the full artwork. Never promise every required detail will survive every thumbnail crop.

Retain the static PNGs and assemble the three Reel/TikTok layouts into eight-second MP4s using user-selected approved music; follow [Reel video production](reel-video.md). Verify account-level upload/boost eligibility and actual placement previews separately from technical video validation. Do not claim that exporting an MP4 establishes ad eligibility.

## Copy and link behavior

These lengths are editorial targets, not asserted platform limits. Put the hook and distinguishing show fact first; facts and CTA should survive caption truncation. Use short paragraphs and avoid repeated CTAs, keyword stuffing, or invented urgency.

| Placement | Writing direction | Link/CTA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook post | About 40–100 words; clear invitation, local venue, date/time; optional 0–3 relevant hashtags | Include `https://improv-circus.com` directly in the organic post. Check link rendering before publication. |
| Facebook Reel | About 20–60 words; short hook, date/time and venue; optional 0–3 hashtags | Prefer “Details at improv-circus.com”; do not depend on a Reel caption URL being clickable. Paid version uses website CTA button. |
| Instagram post | About 40–100 words; compelling first line, short readable paragraphs, 0–5 relevant hashtags | Bio variant: “Show details and tickets via the link in bio.” Use only after setting/checking the bio link to the main website. Fallback: “Details at improv-circus.com.” Do not imply ordinary caption URLs are clickable. |
| Instagram Reel | About 20–60 words; concise complementary caption, avoid merely repeating every graphic line | Same bio/fallback rule as Instagram feed; paid version refers to website button rather than bio. |
| TikTok | Aim for 1–2 short sentences, ideally under 150 characters for a short variant when required facts fit; otherwise favor accurate facts over an arbitrary length | Use “Details at improv-circus.com” or a bio variant conditional on an available configured website link. Do not rely on a caption URL being clickable. |

Paid/boosted alternatives: short hook, factual event summary, one “Learn more” or appropriate available website-button CTA. Button destination is `https://improv-circus.com`. Do not invent a configured button or claim an ad has been set up. For TikTok Non-Spark captions, avoid clickable-link claims, @ mentions, and hashtags per the cited ad specification; organic/Spark copy can differ. Keep captions short because overlays reduce the usable artwork area. Overlay copy should use 2–4 short lines emphasizing show, date/time and CTA, within the artwork safe zone; it must not cover existing required details. Event blurbs should stand alone. Alt text should prioritize show identity and factual displayed details over decorative adjectives.

## Source record and verification limits

Official sources checked during skill creation and the 2026-10-11 safe-area review:

- [Meta-authored Reels creative guide, page 4](https://d3m889aznlr23d.cloudfront.net/img/events/458925814/assets/e042d2be.reels_ads_guide1.pdf): recommends reserving the bottom 35% of Reels ads for UI. This accessible copy supports the Instagram bottom reserve; it does not verify a universal organic safe rectangle or the current top/side percentages.

- [TikTok auction in-feed specifications](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en): vertical 9:16 recommended, video formats, caption behavior, and safe-zone dependency on dimensions, caption length and additional formats. TikTok supplies different downloadable overlays for standard/anchor placements and language direction. Exact paid layout must be checked against the matching overlay and posting preview; the table above is intentionally a conservative house approximation.
- [TikTok creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en): vertical composition, safe-zone visibility, hook, distinguishing benefits, and clear CTA.
- [TikTok profile website guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/launch-your-small-business-tiktok-business-account): website links have eligibility requirements; do not assume the account has a configured clickable website field or hard-code eligibility thresholds from an older article.
- [Meta Facebook feed image guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/facebook-feed), [Instagram feed image guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-feed), and [Meta Stories/Reels safe-zone help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490): these official pages redirected to login during research. Their precise current recommendations could not be independently verified. The Meta dimensions, margins, file cap, and link conventions above are production choices, not a claim of verified current Meta requirements. Preview each target placement before boosting; refresh this reference if accessible official guidance contradicts it.

Do not claim that a preview proves live rendering on every device. Surface any unresolved crop or eligibility limitation with the review set.
