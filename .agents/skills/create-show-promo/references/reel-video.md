# Eight-second Reel production

## Choose music

All `.mp3` and `.mp4` files in `marketing/Assets/20_audio/` are approved audio options. The `.mp4` files in that directory contain audio only; list them alongside the MP3s in the audio-choice prompt, using their actual filenames and extensions. Do not exclude an option because it has an MP4 extension. Ask the user to choose from the complete list. Probe the selected file for decoding and encoding details, not to decide whether it belongs in the approved option list. Additional approved audio may be found elsewhere under `marketing`; inspect audio-bearing containers there as needed. If none exist, ask for an approved file. Do not synthesize music, download replacements, or substitute an unchosen track. Use the same chosen track and offset for the coordinated three-video set unless the user requests different music. Approval of a source asset does not itself establish platform music-license/boost eligibility; mention an unresolved actual licensing question when relevant, without inventing a rights review requirement.

## Analyze the selected track

Decode to PCM for analysis, keeping the source untouched. Measure short-window RMS energy, onset strength (prefer spectral flux when available), and local/8-second sustained energy. Compare several candidate entrances where an audible rhythmic or melodic section picks up, ideally a downbeat or strong note after a build. Reject isolated clicks, silence, clipped transients, weak introductions and excerpts without eight seconds remaining. Compare/listen to short candidate excerpts when playback inspection is available. Energy/onset measures are proxies: do not claim exact beat/key recognition or listening that was not performed. Record candidate times, selected offset in seconds, evidence, and any listening limitation. Start on the chosen attack without a fade-in that blunts it; a short end fade (about 0.2–0.3 seconds) may smooth the cut. Do not loop short tracks silently; ask for another track or an explicit exception.

## Encode and verify

Use FFmpeg or equivalent local media tools. For each matching Reel PNG, hold the full image for exactly eight seconds, with no pan, zoom, crop, animation or extra overlay unless requested. Preserve the existing dimensions and aspect ratio. Newly generated artwork and any requested promotional overlays must omit the year; do not alter previously approved artwork during a video-only conversion unless the user requests that change. Output MP4 with H.264, yuv420p, square pixels, constant 30 fps (240 frames), AAC audio at 48 kHz, and fast-start metadata. Use a quality setting such as CRF 18. Preserve source dynamics; attenuate if necessary to prevent clipping, and do not add arbitrary gain. Trim the chosen music from the recorded offset to eight seconds. Match the MP4 stem to its PNG (`facebook-reel.mp4`, `instagram-reel.mp4`, `tiktok-reel.mp4`, or an explicitly retained campaign suffix). Write staged outputs first, verify, then replace prior videos in place. Do not create revision folders. Preserve explicitly retained alternatives and unrelated user files.

Verify with ffprobe: H.264 video, AAC audio, expected dimensions, 30 fps, 240 frames, square pixels and approximately 8.000-second duration (allow container/audio encoder rounding up to 0.05 seconds). Decode the full file to check errors. Inspect beginning/middle/end frames for unchanged artwork and verify extracted audio against the intended source excerpt, including start timing and any end fade. Confirm both streams exist and the audio is not silent or clipped. Record checks and limitations. Include video links and the track/offset in posting instructions outside copy.docx. Always obtain approval of the new videos; prior image approval remains valid when those images are unchanged. Do not publish or boost.
