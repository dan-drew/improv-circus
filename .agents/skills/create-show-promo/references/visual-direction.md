# Visual direction: Q426 theatrical poster standard

## Inspect the actual references

Open both `marketing/Promos/Q426 shows 9x16.png` and `marketing/Promos/Q426 shows 4x5.png` before generating. Use them as visual references for finish, energy, and integration. Their show listings and promotional claims are historical/reference material, not sources of current show facts. In particular, “Special Puppets vs Humans show,” “All Ages,” and any other reference-only wording require support from the resolved current brief before reuse. Adapt the visual language to one show; do not reproduce the multi-show schedule.

## What to carry forward

- Oversized Improv Circus and show identity integrated into the scene; the show logo is the hero, not a small badge beside a block of copy.
- Saturated red velvet, warm gold bulbs, electric blue spotlights, dimensional stars, and rich contrast. Light should connect the brand marks and event signage to the theater scene.
- Sculpted marquee frames, illuminated borders, cream sign faces, red/gold ribbons, and playful theatrical display lettering. Date/time should feel like part of the poster's main attraction.
- Foreground and background depth: curtains, spotlights, signs, stars, audience, and guest artwork occupy intentional layers. Select a few strong devices instead of crowding every space.
- Expressive family-entertainment imagery. Inspect approved `marketing/Assets/happy people.png` when an audience treatment suits the concept; do not imply depicted people are the lineup or actual show attendees.
- Purposeful guest integration: a featured marquee medallion, guest stage/sign, or coherent comic-theater crossover with the supplied guest artwork. Keep the original guest headshot/team logo identifiable and intact rather than pasting a small unrelated square into a dashboard-like panel.

These are creative criteria, not a fixed template or a requirement to include every motif. A new show can have its own color accents and guest-driven visual idea while matching the references' production quality.

Integrate letterforms with curved ribbons, sign faces, perspective, shadows and lighting. Validate organic and boosted placement previews separately. Generate layout adaptations rather than distorting portions of a background or stretching/repeating scenery. ## Visual acceptance

Compare the finished image and phone-width preview with both Q426 references. Typography must belong to the sign surfaces, with coherent scale, alignment, perspective and lighting. Brand/show/date dominate; guest and website remain readable. Reject blank banners, lettering crossing sign borders, excessive empty sign space, tiny floating hero logos, detached guest tiles, uniformly outlined text and decorative elements stronger than the show. Safe bounds and correct dimensions alone do not establish visual quality. Check factual text word for word and repair incorrect lettering before presenting the set.
