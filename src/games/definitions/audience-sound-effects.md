## How to Play

“While the players play a scene, a volunteer player will provide all of the sound effects needed.”

The referee asks for a volunteer player from the audience, gets a suggestion for the game, and then the players begin a neutral scene. Whenever the players hear a sound effect they must justify it into the scene, and they may also prompt for specific sounds as well.
