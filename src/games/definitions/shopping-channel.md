## How to Play

1 player is the cheesy infomercial shopping channel host, and the other players mime (no gibberish) clues for what they're selling and 1-2 changes.

Ref sends guesser out of the room and gets 3 everyday items that the guesser will be trying to sell. Then asks for replacements for 2 things (“pencil, the eraser is Spongebob Squarepants, and instead of paper it writes on unicorns”)

Guesser keeps playing to the audience as the informercial through the whole thing while trying to guess what they’re selling.
