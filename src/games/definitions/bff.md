## How to Play

The players deliver reasons to be a volunteer’s best friend, one word at a time, in this kid-friendly variation of Casanova.

The referee asks for a volunteer player from the audience, and the teams line up on either side of the volunteer player. The referee gets a suggestion for a game and chooses a team to start. The first team delivers a line, one word at a time based on the suggestion, on why they should be best friends. Then the second team also delivers a line.

The volunteer player chooses which they prefer, and the first player on each side rotates to the end of their line. The whole process repeats 3-5 times with a new suggestion each time.
